🗣️ Behavioral Questions

Choose a behavioral interview question to review its story and follow-up questions.

20 Questions

  1. Tell me about a time you solved a difficult/challenging production bug.
  2. Tell me about a time you failed and what you learned from it.
  3. What is your greatest strength and your biggest weakness?
  4. Tell me about a time you disagreed with a manager or technical decision.
  5. Tell me about a time you adjusted priorities to meet changing demands.
  6. Give an example of a conflict on a team and how you handled it.
  7. Tell me about a time you took ownership outside your responsibility.
  8. Tell me about a missed deadline or late delivery.
  9. Tell me about a time you had to learn something quickly.
  10. Tell me about a time you received critical feedback.
  11. Describe a challenging bug or performance issue you resolved.
  12. Tell me about a time you improved performance or code quality.
  13. How do you handle unclear or changing requirements?
  14. Tell me about a project that did not go as planned.
  15. Tell me about balancing quality with delivery speed.
  16. How do you approach an unfamiliar codebase or problem?
  17. Tell me about the toughest decision you made recently.
  18. Tell me about a time you stepped into a leadership role.
  19. Describe a disagreement with a team member and how you resolved it.
  20. Tell me about communicating a technical risk to a non-technical stakeholder.
